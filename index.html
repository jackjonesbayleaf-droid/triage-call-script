Front Door / Triage  ·  Juanita Peters
Step 1

Interruption phrases

Use any time during the call. Say the phrase, pause, then immediately ask your next question.

Quick pause: "Can I stop you there?" — wait — then continue: "What I need to know is…"
Or: "I'm just going to pause you for a moment." — then ask your next question immediately.

Voicemail script

"Hello, this is Juanita Peters from the Front Door Team at CAMHS Bedfordshire.

I was hoping to speak with [Parent Name] regarding a referral we have received.

If you would like to contact us before my next call attempt, please contact CAMHS reception on 01525 638 613 and ask for a message to be passed to the Front Door Team requesting a call back.

Otherwise, I will make one further attempt to contact you. Again, that number for CAMHS reception is 01525 638 613.

Thank you."
